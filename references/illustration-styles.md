# Japanese Editorial Illustration

Use the bundled images in `assets/style-references/` as visual references for medium, information density, and layout rhythm only.

## Core Look

- Warm white or clean white editorial-paper background.
- Refined adult fashion illustration with thin ink lines and soft watercolor or marker shading.
- One dominant full-body model with believable anatomy and garment proportions.
- Mixed serif, clean sans-serif, and restrained handwritten English accents.
- Product cutouts in cards or taped-paper frames.
- Hand-drawn arrows, brackets, circles, underlines, check marks, and short notes.
- Four to six labeled color swatches.

## Layout Families

### Daily Coordinate

Use a large central model, vertical item cards, checklist note, palette, and styling memo. Best for a complete outfit.

### Street Layered

Use stronger black or blue accents, boxed item cards, accessory strip, and energetic hand-drawn marks. Best for denim, streetwear, layered vests, boots, or graphic tops.

### Monochrome Editorial

Use generous whitespace, large English serif title, restrained accent color, fabric macro panels, and isolated product cutouts. Best for black, white, beige, and minimalist outfits.

### Focus Accessory

Use one oversized scarf, bag, belt, or shoe cutout with a close-up of how it is styled, supported by smaller garment cards.

## Product Authority

The product-authority image always overrides these style references. Copy only composition, drawing medium, note style, and information density. Do not copy reference garments, silhouettes, pockets, trims, logos, accessories, Japanese text, or colorways.

## English Copy

Use short sections such as `COORDINATE 01`, `STYLING POINT`, `ITEMS IN THIS LOOK`, `DETAIL`, `COLOR PALETTE`, `ACCESSORIES`, and `STYLE MEMO`. Keep each note to one short sentence or two short lines.
