# Model Mode Details

## Multiple Variants

When creating Chinese, Korean, and Malay Hijab versions of one outfit:

1. Use the same product authority image in every call.
2. Keep title, product labels, palette, product cutouts, and layout consistent unless requested otherwise.
3. Change only model identity, hair or hijab treatment, makeup, and modest base layer.
4. Generate each variant in a separate call.
5. Compare variants for matching garment length, silhouette, texture, and hardware.

## Fixed Character Continuity

Reuse the most recent approved model image as the series reference. Lock identity, face shape, skin tone, hijab style or hairstyle, body proportions, and illustration medium.

For photorealistic series, also lock lens feel, lighting direction, skin treatment, hair texture, and retouching level. Preserve natural skin texture instead of reproducing plastic smoothing from a rejected draft.

## Modest Adaptation Priority

1. Preserve the product unchanged.
2. Cover exposed skin with a fitted neutral inner layer.
3. Coordinate the hijab without obscuring the neckline or garment front.
4. If coverage requires materially redesigning the product, show the product accurately in an isolated item card and make the model adaptation explicit.

Never describe an added inner layer as part of the sold product unless the source confirms it.

## Korean Photorealistic

- Use a real young adult Korean woman with natural contemporary hair and makeup.
- Style a supplied scarf around the neck or shoulders unless the user requests another use.
- Keep hands outside absent or unconfirmed pockets.
- Do not use oversized eyes, porcelain skin, a doll face, or illustration-like hair.

## Malay Hijab Photorealistic

- Use a real young adult Malay Muslim woman with natural Malaysian features and realistic skin texture.
- Use the supplied scarf as the hijab when it is suitable, preserving its exact color, texture, and size impression.
- Cover all hair and neck with no stray hair. Keep the hijab simple and do not hide the neckline, pintucks, buttons, ties, or other focus construction.
- Do not add an inner layer when the original long-sleeve outfit already provides full coverage.
- Keep the product unchanged; modesty adaptation applies only to styling and required base coverage.
