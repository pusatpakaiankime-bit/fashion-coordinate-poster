# Layout Selection

Choose one pattern based on the outfit rather than repeating one poster.

## Editorial Detail

Use for texture, hardware, or unusual construction: central model, product cutouts on the left, two macro panels on the right, styling note near the title, and palette at the bottom.

## Item Check

Use for a complete coordinated look: central or offset model, vertical item-card stack, taped-paper checklist, arrows, and four-color palette.

## Mix & Match

Use for casual, street, Korean, or accessory-led looks: model on one side, `ITEMS IN THIS LOOK` strip at the top, three detail callouts, large opposite-side title, keywords, and palette.

## Focus Item

Use when one garment is the main product: large hero model, oversized focus-item cutout or detail, smaller supporting-item cards, short checklist, and up to six palette colors.

Keep no more than five visual groups across one horizontal row. Preserve generous whitespace.
